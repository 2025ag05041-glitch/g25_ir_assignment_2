Put the BITS Virtual Lab screenshots (.png or .jpg) in this folder.
Name them in the order they should appear, for example:
  01_vlab_terminal_install.png
  02_vlab_streamlit_running.png
  03_vlab_dashboard.png
  ...
Then run:  python scripts/build_report.py
and the screenshots are added to Appendix A of the report automatically.

Put the screen recording (e.g. G25_demo_recording.mp4) in the evidence/ folder.
See the "BITS Virtual Lab checklist" section in README.md.
